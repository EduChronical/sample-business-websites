FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking an appointment online or on WhatsApp guarantees your slot and reduces waiting time.

What is the consultation fee?

General consultation starts at ₹300 and specialist consultation at ₹500. Follow-up visits within 7 days are free.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes, we offer cashless treatment with most major insurers and TPAs. Please carry your insurance card and ID proof.

Is home sample collection available?

Yes, free home sample collection is available within Vizianagaram city for lab orders above ₹500.
