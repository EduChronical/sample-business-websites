Our Story

Recipes from grandmother's kitchen, served with love

Spice Route Kitchen began as a small family eatery with one goal — to serve the bold, honest flavours of Andhra home cooking. Today our chefs blend coastal Andhra traditions with Hyderabadi and Mughlai classics, using spices we roast and grind in-house every morning.
